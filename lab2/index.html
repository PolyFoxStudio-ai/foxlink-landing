Questions answered

The bits people ask.

How does the free trial work?

Sign up with your email — no card needed. You get 30 days of full access to every feature, including all the AI tools. After the trial, upgrade to Pro or your data stays safe and you can come back any time.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are up and running in under an hour. Import your contacts from CSV, set your pipeline stages, and you're selling the same day. No consultant, no lengthy onboarding.

What is the daily brief?

FoxLink's AI writes you a personalised briefing every morning — your top-priority contacts ranked by urgency, deals closing soon, overdue tasks, and what the team logged yesterday. It arrives before 7am, and you can toggle it off any time in Settings.

Is my data safe and GDPR compliant?

Yes. FoxLink runs on UK-hosted infrastructure in the London region with row-level security, so your workspace data is completely isolated. We're registered with the ICO and comply fully with UK GDPR. We never sell or share your data.

Why BCC instead of connecting my whole inbox?

By design, for privacy. Full-inbox integrations pull in every email you send and receive — including messages about people who never agreed to sit in a CRM. FoxLink only sees the emails you deliberately BCC or forward, so nothing lands in your workspace without a conscious decision.

We already have a CRM.

Most teams do. The question is whether your team actually uses it. FoxLink is built to be the CRM your reps open every morning — not the one they update when they have to. Try it for 30 days and see.

Can it integrate with our other tools?

Yes. Connect thousands of apps through Zapier, or build directly against our REST API with signed webhooks. CSV import and export are always available too, so your data is never locked in.

What happens when my trial ends?

Upgrade to Pro any time from Settings → Billing. If you don't, you move to read-only access — your data is safe and you can come back whenever you're ready.
