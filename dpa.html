Last updated: August 2026 · Polyfox Studio Ltd · Registered in England & Wales

In short: When you use FoxLink to store data about your own contacts, you are the data controller and we are your processor. This agreement sets out how we handle that data on your behalf under UK GDPR. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

  • the customer identified in the Agreement (the "Customer", "you", the "Controller"),

each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

This DPA reflects the Parties' agreement on the processing of Personal Data in connection with the FoxLink CRM service (the "Service"). Where this DPA conflicts with the Agreement on the subject of data protection, this DPA prevails.

1. Definitions

1.1. "Data Protection Laws" means all laws applicable to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, including the UK GDPR (the retained EU Regulation 2016/679 as it forms part of UK law under the Data Protection Act 2018), the Data Protection Act 2018, and, where applicable, the EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679).

1.2. "Controller", "Processor", "Data Subject", "Personal Data", "Personal Data Breach", "Processing", and "Supervisory Authority" have the meanings given to them in the Data Protection Laws.

1.3. "Customer Personal Data" means Personal Data contained within the Customer content that FoxLink processes on the Customer's behalf under the Agreement.

1.4. "Sub-processor" means any Processor engaged by FoxLink to process Customer Personal Data.

1.5. "Standard Contractual Clauses" or "SCCs" means, as applicable, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum ("UK IDTA") issued under section 119A of the Data Protection Act 2018, and/or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for the transfer of personal data to third countries (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914).

1.6. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Agreement.

2. Roles and scope of processing

2.1. The Parties acknowledge that, for Customer Personal Data, the Customer is the Controller and FoxLink is the Processor.

2.2. FoxLink acts as an independent Controller in respect of the account, authentication, and billing data of the Customer's own authorised users, and its processing of that data is governed by FoxLink's Privacy Policy rather than this DPA.

2.3. The subject-matter, duration, nature, and purpose of the processing, the types of Personal Data, and the categories of Data Subjects are set out in Annex I.

2.4. This DPA applies for as long as FoxLink processes Customer Personal Data on the Customer's behalf.

3. Customer obligations

3.1. The Customer warrants that it has a lawful basis to provide Customer Personal Data to FoxLink and to instruct the processing described in this DPA, and that it has provided all notices and obtained all consents required under Data Protection Laws.

3.2. The Customer is responsible for the accuracy, quality, and legality of Customer Personal Data and the means by which it acquired that data.

3.3. The Customer's complete and final instructions for the processing of Customer Personal Data are set out in the Agreement, this DPA, and the configuration options the Customer selects within the Service. Any additional instruction requires prior written agreement.

4. FoxLink obligations

FoxLink shall:

4.1. Documented instructions. Process Customer Personal Data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including with regard to international transfers, unless required to do otherwise by applicable law; in which case FoxLink shall inform the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits such notification on important grounds of public interest.

4.2. Confidentiality. Ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data are bound by an appropriate obligation of confidentiality.

4.3. Security. Implement and maintain the technical and organisational measures set out in Annex II, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing.

4.4. Sub-processors. Engage Sub-processors only in accordance with clause 5.

4.5. Data-subject requests. Taking into account the nature of the processing, assist the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as possible, to respond to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under Data Protection Laws. The Service provides self-service tooling for erasure (see clause 4.9 and Annex II) that the Customer may use directly. If FoxLink receives a request from a Data Subject relating to Customer Personal Data, it shall, unless legally prohibited, promptly forward the request to the Customer and not respond to it directly except on the Customer's instructions.

4.6. Breach notification. Notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data, and provide the Customer with information reasonably required to meet the Customer's own breach-notification obligations.

4.7. DPIAs. Provide reasonable assistance to the Customer with data-protection impact assessments and prior consultations with Supervisory Authorities, taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to FoxLink.

4.8. Audits. Make available to the Customer information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and, on reasonable prior written notice and no more than once per twelve (12) months (or following a Personal Data Breach, or where required by a Supervisory Authority), allow for and contribute to audits conducted by the Customer or an independent auditor mandated by the Customer, subject to reasonable confidentiality and security conditions. FoxLink may satisfy this obligation by providing relevant third-party certifications or reports where available.

4.9. Deletion or return. At the Customer's choice, delete or return all Customer Personal Data after the end of the provision of the Service, and delete existing copies unless applicable law requires storage. The Customer may also delete Customer Personal Data at any time during the term using the Service's in-app erasure and workspace-deletion tools. Residual copies in routine backups are deleted in the ordinary course of the backup rotation.

6.2. 7.2. This DPA takes effect on the effective date of the Agreement and continues until FoxLink has ceased all processing of Customer Personal Data and deleted or returned it in accordance with clause 4.9.

Annex II — Technical and organisational measures

FoxLink maintains the following measures. These reflect the Service as operated on the date of this DPA and may be updated to maintain an equivalent or higher level of security.

1. Access control and authentication

  • Role-based access control with defined workspace roles (owner, admin, manager, member); administrative capabilities (member and role management, workspace settings, integrations, billing) are restricted by role and enforced server-side.
  • Database-level Row Level Security isolates each workspace's data.
  • Authentication is provided via a managed authentication provider.

2. Encryption

  • Personal Data is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Personal Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 at the storage-platform layer.

3. Secrets and integration security

  • API keys are stored only as hashed digests (SHA-256); the plaintext key is shown to the user once and is not recoverable. Keys are revocable and rate-limited.
  • Outbound webhooks are signed with an HMAC-SHA256 signature so recipients can verify authenticity.

4. Logging and monitoring

  • An immutable per-workspace audit log records administrative and data-changing actions, retained for twelve (12) months.
  • Automated monitoring reviews workspace activity and authentication for anomalous patterns (including bulk deletions, mass data export, and repeated failed logins) and alerts our security team, so that a suspected incident is investigated promptly and any confirmed Personal Data Breach is notified within the timeframe in clause 4.6.

5. Data segregation

  • Customer Personal Data is logically segregated per workspace.

6. Sub-processor infrastructure

  • Hosting, database, storage, email delivery, payment processing, and AI processing are provided by the Sub-processors in Annex III, each of which maintains its own certified security programme.

7. Backups and resilience

  • Automated daily backups of the database are retained for 7 days, providing a recovery point objective (RPO) of up to 24 hours. Higher-frequency point-in-time recovery is available and may be enabled as the Service scales.

8. AI processing safeguards

  • AI features are provided solely via Anthropic. Customer Personal Data sent to Anthropic for processing is not used to train Anthropic's models, consistent with Anthropic's commercial API terms.

9. Data deletion

  • The Service provides self-service erasure of individual member data and deletion of entire workspaces; deletion propagates to remove associated Personal Data, with residual backup copies removed in the ordinary backup rotation.
