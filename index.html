Features

Built for teams who sell.
Not teams who manage software.

Every feature in FoxLink earns its place. Nothing is there to look good on a comparison chart — it's there because it saves you time.

📋
Deals
Your sales pipeline on one drag-and-drop board. Every deal card carries a value, a stage, and a close date — feeding your forecasts and sales reports automatically. Health indicators flag anything going quiet or slipping past its close date.
👥
Contacts & activity
Unlimited contacts linked to companies, with full activity history, AI summaries and lead scores. Log calls, emails and meetings in seconds, and import your whole list from CSV in minutes.
✅
Tasks
Create tasks linked to contacts, deals, or workflow cards. Assign to team members. Overdue tasks surface at the top every morning so nothing gets missed. AI ranks your day around them.
🗓️
Calendar
A full calendar with day, week, month, and agenda views. Create events, invite contacts and team members, and see all your tasks alongside your meetings in one place.
🗂️
Workflows
Up to four custom Kanban boards for the work that isn't a sale — onboarding, delivery, support, hiring. Each has its own columns and a task board per card, and AI tracks progress against the goal you set. Kept out of your sales reports.
🗃️
Boards
Monday.com-style custom tables for the data a pipeline can't hold — typed columns, colour-coded groups, and AI columns that summarise or categorise each row. View any board as a table, Kanban, or calendar, right beside your deals and contacts.
📬
Web intake forms
Build a lead capture form and embed it on your website. Submissions land directly in your CRM as new contacts — no extra tools, no manual import, no delay.
📨
Email to CRM
Every user gets a private BCC address. Forward or BCC a client email and FoxLink logs it against the right contact automatically — anything it can't match waits in a review queue for one-click assignment. No mailbox to connect, no extension to install.
👤
Team workspace
Invite your team with roles — owner, admin, manager, member — so the right people control settings and billing while everyone shares one pipeline. An audit log tracks every change.
📊
Reports & exports
Pipeline reports, contact lists, company summaries, and activity logs — all exportable as PDF or CSV in seconds. Always up to date, no manual work required.
🔌
Zapier, API & webhooks
Connect to thousands of apps via Zapier, or build directly against the REST API with signed webhooks that fire in real time. Generate a key in Settings and you're wired up in minutes.
📱
Desktop & mobile
Full-featured on desktop, with a focused mobile experience for logging calls, checking today's priorities, and tapping to call or email while you're on the road. Light and dark themes throughout — pick whichever suits your day.

FAQ

Questions answered.

How does the free trial work?
Sign up with your email — no card needed. You get 30 days of full access to every feature including all AI tools. After the trial, upgrade to Pro or your data stays safe and you can come back any time.
How long does setup take?
Most teams are up and running in under an hour. Import your contacts from CSV, configure your pipeline stages, and you're selling the same day. No consultant required, no lengthy onboarding.
What is the daily agenda email?
FoxLink's AI writes you a personalised briefing every morning — your top-priority contacts ranked by urgency, deals closing soon, overdue tasks, and what your team logged yesterday. Arrives before 7am. Toggle it off any time in Settings.
How do web intake forms work?
Build a form in FoxLink and embed it on your website. When someone fills it in, they're automatically added as a contact in your workspace — no Zapier, no manual import, no delay.
Is my data safe and GDPR compliant?
Yes. FoxLink runs on UK-hosted infrastructure in the London region with row-level security — your workspace data is completely isolated. We're registered with the ICO and comply fully with UK GDPR. We never sell or share your data.
Why BCC instead of connecting my whole inbox?
By design, for privacy. Full-inbox integrations pull in every email you send and receive — including messages about people who never agreed to sit in a CRM. FoxLink only sees the emails you deliberately BCC or forward, so nothing lands in your workspace without a conscious decision — data minimisation built in, not bolted on. There's also no mailbox password or OAuth token for us to store.
We already have a CRM.
Most teams do. The question is whether your team actually uses it. FoxLink is built to be the CRM your reps open every morning — not the one they update when they have to. Try it for 30 days and see.
Can it integrate with our other tools?
Yes. Connect FoxLink to thousands of apps through our Zapier integration, or build directly against our REST API with signed webhooks for real-time events — generate an API key in Settings to get started. CSV import and export are always available too, so your data is never locked in. (Native calendar sync is still on the roadmap — tell us what you need and you'll influence what comes next.)
What happens when my trial ends?
You can upgrade to Pro at any time from Settings → Billing. If you don't upgrade, you'll move to read-only access — your data is safe and you can come back whenever you're ready.
