FoxLink email integrations — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

FoxLink for Gmail (a browser extension) and FoxLink for Outlook (an Office add-in) — each referred to below as “the extension” — are published by Polyfox Studio Ltd (company no. 17227391), 66 Paul Street, London EC2A 4NA, United Kingdom. It is a companion to your FoxLink CRM account. This policy explains exactly what the extension accesses, what it sends, and what it does not do.

The short version. The extension reads only what it needs to identify the person you’re emailing — the sender’s email address and name, and the email subject — from the Gmail or Outlook message you’re viewing. It sends the sender’s email address to your FoxLink account to find the matching contact, and it sends data you explicitly choose to save (an activity, task, deal, calendar event or a contact edit). It does not sync your mailbox, read message bodies, or scan your inbox in the background. It contains no advertising, tracking or analytics, and never sells your data.

What the extension accesses

  • In the Gmail or Outlook message you have open: the sender’s email address and display name, and the subject line of the open (or highlighted) message. This is read only while you’re viewing it, to match or create a FoxLink contact. Message bodies are not read.
  • Your FoxLink sign-in: you sign in on the FoxLink web app (app.foxlink.network), not inside the extension. A small helper reads the resulting session so the extension can act as you. Your session token is stored locally and sent as an authorization header to the FoxLink API. It is never exposed to the Gmail or Outlook page, and the extension never handles your password.

What the extension sends, and to whom

All network requests go to your FoxLink application (app.foxlink.network) and its backend. Specifically:

  • The sender’s email address, to look up the matching contact and their open deals, workflows and recent activity.
  • Data you choose to create or edit: logged activities (call/note/meeting/email), tasks, deals, calendar events, contact details and links.
  • Your daily brief and task list, retrieved to display the Home and Tasks tabs.

No data is sent to any third party other than the infrastructure that already runs FoxLink — see the FoxLink privacy policy and subprocessors list. FoxLink’s backend is hosted in the EU/UK.

Data retention

The extension stores only your session token locally, to keep you signed in. It holds no other data at rest. Anything you save is stored in your FoxLink account under your workspace and is governed by the main FoxLink privacy policy; you can delete it there. Signing out of the extension clears the stored token.

Permissions, and why

  • Local storage — to keep your FoxLink session token so you stay signed in.
  • Access to your inbox (Gmail / Outlook) — to show the panel and read the sender and subject of the message you’re viewing (the Gmail extension reads the open message; the Outlook add-in uses Office’s read-item permission). Message bodies aren’t read in the background.
  • Access to app.foxlink.network and the FoxLink backend — to sign in and to read/write your CRM data on your behalf.

Your rights

FoxLink is operated under UK GDPR. You can access, correct, export or delete your data from within FoxLink, or by contacting us. For data-subject requests or any privacy question, contact info@polyfox.studio.

Changes

We’ll update this policy as the extension evolves and revise the date above.
