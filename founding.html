The FoxLink Founding 10

Help shape FoxLink · 90 days of full Pro, free

We're a small team, and we'd rather build FoxLink around ten real businesses than a thousand anonymous signups. So we're inviting a handful of companies to become Founding Members — and to help us make FoxLink genuinely indispensable.

This isn't a discount code or a bigger free trial dressed up. It's a working relationship: you use FoxLink for real, we stay close, and what you tell us shapes what we build next.

What you get

  • 90 days of full FoxLink Pro, free — every feature, all the AI. No card required.
  • Personal onboarding. We set FoxLink up around how you actually work, rather than handing you an empty account.
  • A direct line to the founders. Reply to any email and it comes straight to us — not a support queue.
  • Real influence. Founding Members help steer the roadmap. The things you find missing are the things we build.
  • Founding Member status — you were one of the first, and we'll always treat you that way.

What we ask in return

One thing: your honest experience. What works, what doesn't, what's missing, what you'd never touch. Blunt is better than polite — "this annoyed me" is far more useful to us than "this is great."

We will never require a review. If FoxLink genuinely earns it, we may later ask whether you'd be happy to share your experience — but that's always your call, always after real use, and never a condition of the programme.

Who we're looking for

  • Small businesses, typically 2–20 people.
  • You have customers, prospects, or deals to keep on top of.
  • You've a repeatable way of selling or managing customers — even if it's currently spreadsheets, email, or a CRM you've outgrown.
  • Someone (owner, manager, or sales lead) who's up for engaging with us over the 90 days.
  • An openness to giving candid feedback.

We deliberately keep the group small, and we cherry-pick for fit — so this is an application, not an instant signup. If it's not the right moment, you can always start a normal 30-day free trial instead.

How it works

  • You apply — a short form, a few honest answers.
  • We review — we read every one and pick a small number that are a genuine fit.
  • We get you set up — a personal onboarding around your actual process, aiming for a real first win, not a feature tour.
  • We stay close for 90 days — occasional check-ins, and a founder on the other end whenever you need one.
